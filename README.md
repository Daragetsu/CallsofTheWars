** Calls of The Wars **

an addon mod for the Scorched Guns mod that adds new conflict grounds and mobs,

players are called forth to the wars to help protect the peace of the worlds across the universe,

players will be sent in as one of the 3 main groups(Red, Green or Blue),

and they can fight against the other 2 groups and many other threats shoulder to shoulder with their groups,

players will be rewarded based on their battle field performance,

so what are you waiting for? go and win!
